Ways to Get Involved

There are many ways to participate in your child's education:

  • Attend parent-teacher conferences
  • Join the PTA or PTO
  • Volunteer in the classroom
  • Help with homework and projects
  • Participate in school events

Communication with Teachers

Effective communication between parents and teachers creates a strong support system for students. Here are some best practices:

  1. Check the school's communication portal regularly
  2. Respond promptly to teacher emails or calls
  3. Schedule meetings when you have concerns
  4. Be open to feedback about your child's progress
  5. Share relevant information about your child's needs
