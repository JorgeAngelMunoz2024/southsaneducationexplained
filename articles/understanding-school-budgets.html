Understanding School Budgets

Published: August 2026

Introduction to School Funding

School budgets can be complex and difficult to understand. This article breaks down the key components of how schools are funded and where the money goes.

Understanding your school district's budget is crucial for informed participation in local education decisions. When you know how funds are allocated, you can better advocate for the needs of students and teachers.

Main Sources of Funding

School districts typically receive funding from three main sources:

  1. Local property taxes
  2. State education funds
  3. Federal grants and programs

Budget Categories

District budgets are typically divided into several major categories:

  • Instructional costs (teacher salaries, classroom supplies)
  • Administrative expenses (district office, support staff)
  • Facilities and maintenance (building upkeep, utilities)
  • Transportation (school buses, fuel)
  • Special programs (special education, ESL, gifted programs)

An informed community is an empowered community. When parents and residents understand the budget, they can work together to ensure every student gets the resources they need.

— Education Budget Expert

How You Can Get Involved

There are several ways you can participate in budget discussions:

  • Attend school board meetings
  • Review published budget documents
  • Join parent-teacher organizations
  • Contact board members with questions
  • Vote in school bond elections
