Welcome to South San Education Explained

South San schools affect students, families, educators, and the entire community. But understanding district decisions - what changed, why it changed, and what the evidence actually shows - is not always easy.
South San Education Explained makes complex district decisions easier to understand, connects readers to the original sources, and provides the context needed to evaluate the information for themselves.

What We Do

We follow South San Antonio ISD board meetings, policies, programs, spending, academic information, and other major district decisions. We break down complex policies, programs, data, and district actions into clear, understandable information and connect readers to the records and sources behind them
