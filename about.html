Our Mission

South San Education Explained is an independent community information project created to make South San Antonio ISD decisions easier to understand. District policies, board actions, budgets, instructional programs, and education data can be complicated - and important information is often spread across meeting videos, agendas, reports, presentations, policies, and public records.
Our mission is to bring that information together, explain it in plain language, and provide the sources so readers can examine the evidence for themselves. 
South San Education Explained was created by a former South San teacher. That experience helps shape the questions we ask, particularly about how district decisions affect classrooms, teachers, and students. We do not claim to have no perspective. We do commit to following the evidence, distinguishing documented facts from interpretation and unanswered questions, and correcting or updating our work when new information becomes available. 

What We Do

We start with a question: What happened, what was said, and what does the available evidence show?
We review public records, Board materials, meeting videos and transcripts, district documents, state information, and other relevant sources. We try to distinguish clearly between documented facts, unanswered questions, and conclusions that the the evidence does - or does not - support.
When important information is unavailable we say so. When new information becomes available, we update our work.

Our Values

  • Accuracy: We verify information and correct or update it when new evidence becomes available.
  • Transparency: We show where information comes from and link to original sources whenever possible.
  • Independence: We are not affiliated with the district and follow the evidence wherever it leads.
  • Community: We focus on the questions and decisions that matter to South San students, families, educators, and residents.

Who We Serve

Our resources are designed for parents, students, educators, and anyone interested in understanding the South San educational system. Whether you're new to the area or a long-time resident, we're here to help you navigate the educational landscape.
