Why Did South San Change How Teachers Teach?

Were Teachers Really What South San Needed to Fix? - Part 1

Published: September 2026

TEA replaced South San’s elected Board primarily to address longstanding governance and management problems. Yet less than two months later, the new Board moved to substantially restructure classroom instruction districtwide by approving NIET/TAP, a management-style model that shifts instructional decision-making toward district-directed expectations and monitoring. What is NIET/TAP—and was deficient teaching really a significant enough problem at South San to warrant such a rapid districtwide restructuring of classroom instruction?
